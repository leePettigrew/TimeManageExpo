# Worker Privacy Notice — Time & Location Tracking

> **Template.** Each employer (data controller) must complete the bracketed
> sections and give this notice to every worker BEFORE they first use the app.
> The app also shows a summary of this notice on first launch and records that
> it was shown. Have a solicitor review before commercial launch.

**Employer / data controller:** [Company name, address, contact]
**App operator / data processor:** [Your trading name, contact email]

## What is collected

- Clock-in and clock-out times, with GPS position at each event
- Your location roughly every 2–5 minutes **only while you are clocked in**
- Phone battery level and GPS accuracy with each location point (to tell a
  dead battery apart from a switched-off tracker)
- Your phone number (sign-in) and device model
- Anomaly indicators (e.g. fake-GPS apps, impossible travel speeds)

## What is NOT collected

- Your location before clock-in, after clock-out, on breaks taken off the
  clock, or on days off. Tracking stops completely at clock-out — the phone's
  own indicators (Android notification, iOS location arrow) confirm this.

## Why (purpose and legal basis)

Verifying working time and site attendance for payroll — the employer's
**legitimate interest** (GDPR Art 6(1)(f)), assessed in a Legitimate Interests
Assessment held by the employer. Location data collected for time verification
will not be used for unrelated purposes (Irish DPC guidance; *Doolin v DPC*).

## Retention

- Location trails: deleted automatically after **90 days**
- Clock times and hour totals: kept as long as payroll law requires
- Manager corrections: kept alongside the original record (audit trail)

## Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure (where applicable), restriction, and objection —
contact [employer contact]. You may complain to the Data Protection Commission
(dataprotection.ie).

## Recipients & processors

Data is stored on [hosting location — e.g. the operator's server in Ireland].
Sub-processors: [SMS provider — e.g. Twilio, for sign-in codes only: phone
numbers, never location]. Data is not sold or shared with anyone other than
your employer.
